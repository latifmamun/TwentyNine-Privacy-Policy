🃏 Privacy Policy

🔒 Overview

Twenty Nine - 29 Card Game ("we", "our", "the Game") is an offline card game developed for entertainment. Your privacy is extremely important to us. This policy explains how we handle information.

Key Point: Our game is 100% offline. We do NOT collect, store, or share any personal information. No internet required to play.

📊 Data Collection

What We DON'T Collect

  • No personal information (name, email, phone)
  • No location data or GPS
  • No contacts or photos
  • No device identifiers for tracking
  • No gameplay data sent to servers

What Is Stored Locally (On Your Device Only)

All data is stored locally using Unity PlayerPrefs and is deleted when you uninstall the game or tap "New Match".

📢 Advertisements

We use Google AdMob to show interstitial ads (after every 4th deal and at match end). AdMob may collect:

  • Advertising ID (for personalized ads, if you allow)
  • Basic device info to serve relevant ads
  • Ad interaction data (clicks, views)
Your Choice: You can opt-out of personalized ads in your device settings: Settings > Google > Ads > Opt out of Ads Personalization. Our game remains fully playable.

AdMob Privacy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Our game is suitable for all ages (Rated Everyone). We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. Since we collect no personal data, there is no risk. The game contains no in-app purchases, no chat, and no external links that could harm children.
